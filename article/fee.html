Payment procedure

If you want to participate in the conference, there are four steps that you need to follow to make a payment in the ICoSIA 2025.

There are different fees that need to be paid to take part in this conference. Participants from Indonesia should pay the domestic participant fee.

Terms and conditions
  1. Participants can only pay the conference fees after receiving an acceptance notification email.
  2. The registration fee includes the following benefits: seminar materials, lunch, morning and afternoon teas, attendance of all technical sessions, and publication (excluding accommodation).
  3. The registration fee is applicable to one person only. Multiple attending authors must register separately, and those not presenting should register as non-presenters.
  4. After verifying your payment, a receipt will be generated from the payment confirmation details.
  5. Payments are nonrefundable.
  6. Payments cannot be made in cash; they must be made via payment gateway or credit card.
  7. Participants are responsible for transfer fees and bank charges.
  8. At least one author for each accepted paper must register as a presenter.
  9. Participants are charged based on their institution/affiliation, not the nationality of the first author.
  10. Submitting an extra paper applies only to the same presenter.
